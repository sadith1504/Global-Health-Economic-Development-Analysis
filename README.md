

## 🌍 Global Health & Economic Development Analysis

### 📌 Overview

This project analyzes the relationship between **health, economic development, and education** across countries worldwide (2000–2019). Using Python data analysis tools, it uncovers how factors like healthcare spending, GDP per capita, and education level influence life expectancy.

---

### 🎯 Objectives

* Examine the relationship between **GDP and life expectancy**
* Analyze the **impact of healthcare spending on population health**
* Explore the **role of education in improving life expectancy**
* Compare **health outcomes across regions and income groups**

---

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Python**
* **Pandas** – data cleaning and preprocessing
* **Matplotlib / Seaborn** – data visualization
* **Jupyter Notebook** – analysis and reporting

---

### 📂 Dataset

* Source: Kaggle (WHO & World Bank data) 
* Time range: 2000 – 2019
* Key features:

  * Life Expectancy
  * GDP per Capita
  * Health Expenditure (% GDP)
  * Education (Schooling years)

---

### 🔍 Key Analysis

* 📈 Healthcare spending vs Life Expectancy
* 🌎 Regional and income-level comparisons
* 📊 Global trends over time (2000–2019)
* ⚖️ Efficiency of healthcare spending
* 🎓 Education impact on health outcomes

---

### 📊 Key Findings

* Healthcare spending and life expectancy show a **positive correlation** 
* Higher-income countries generally have **longer life expectancy**
* Education level strongly influences health outcomes
* Some countries achieve **high efficiency with lower spending**
* Global life expectancy has steadily increased over time
